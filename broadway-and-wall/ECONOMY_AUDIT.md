# ECONOMY AUDIT — Broadway & Wall

Generated by `pnpm audit` · 3 market seeds × 50 sim years · city seed 20261 · shocks injected at month 120 (year 10) · propagation window 15 years.

Every experiment is a paired run: a CONTROL and a TREATMENT on the same seed and the same city, differing in exactly one injected change. Divergence is reported as the peak relative difference inside the propagation window, the month it peaked (the lag), and the difference at the end of the full fifty years.

**Verdicts.** WIRED — transmits in the right direction with material magnitude. WEAK — transmits but barely. BROKEN — nothing moved. BACKWARDS — moved the wrong way, which is worse than nothing.

## Narrative test — three districts, told from the data alone

**exchange.** Office asking rent opened at $54/sf against 80.3% occupancy, on land assessed at $287/sf, in a district whose buildings already averaged 25 years old. It ran to $439 by year 37 with occupancy at 86.7% and citywide office vacancy down at 5.1% — the market was in peak, and the city had been starting 29,684 sf a month of office into it for the previous year. That supply arrived: by year 46 the rent was $52 and occupancy 73.4%, with the market in recovery. Land followed the income it could support, from $287/sf to $607/sf across the fifty years. It finished at $110/sf and 87.7% occupancy against a price level 3.31x its opening, with an average building age of 75 years.
