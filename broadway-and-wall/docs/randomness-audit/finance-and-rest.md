# Fake-number audit: finance and the remaining engine files

Verified against main at 07ddca62 (#839), 2026-11-20.

Read-only first pass against broadway-and-wall/CLAUDE.md "NO FAKE NUMBERS" and "Randomness: only at the boundary of the model". Paths are relative to `broadway-and-wall/src/engine/`. Nothing was run. "Binds?" claims come from the code's own comments or from arithmetic. Where a row says "unverified binding", nobody has measured a bind rate.

**Scope.** Every `src/engine/*.ts` file that is in none of the five existing tables' file lists (market.md, dev.md, leasing.md, rivals.md, propmarket-economy.md). The economically important ones were read: proforma, facility, fund, streetLoans, reputation, supply, industries, industryData, syndicate, raise, waterfall, peers, loanbook, jv, leasehold, space, pricing, products, buybox, capplan, books, standing. The check also found two economic files no table listed: **rivalDebt.ts** (rivals.md quotes one line of it but leaves it out of its scope) and **amortize.ts** (it holds `CAP_PREMIUM_PCT`). Both are covered here. One constant in a covered file is added as well: `CASH_MONTHS` in ownership.ts, which streetLoans.ts reads and which propmarket-economy.md did not list.

**Where the randomness is.** These files hardly draw at all. A grep for `rng(`, `rrange(`, `d(`, `hash01`, `hashNormal`, `hashU01`, gaussians, `jitter`, `noise` and `pick(` finds only three real draw sites:
- `raise.ts:214-226`: a private LCG that runs the raise preview 200 times. It touches no game stream.
- `rivalDebt.ts:406`: a `hash01` that backdates an opening loan's origin month.
- `portfoliosale.ts:741, 821`: already rated in rivals.md.

The other hits are comments. "noise" is a common word in this codebase. So the faults in this table are almost all **asserted levels, asserted forms and load-bearing bounds**, not dice.

**Top 10 fakes**

1. **proforma.ts:880 `LAND_CARRY_RATE = 0.12`**: an unsourced 12% under every land residual in town.
2. **proforma.ts:1152, 1161**: new-build TI and commissions are fixed sheets. The street solves the package from net-effective rent, and the pro forma's rent is already effective, so fit-out may be charged twice.
3. **ownership.ts:433 `CASH_MONTHS`, enforced at streetLoans.ts:358**: a private holder's cash is capped at asserted months of CFADS. That decides when most of the town's mortgages default.
4. **reputation.ts:61-75, 426-452, 518-523, 640-648**: a hand-scored track record, a forced grading curve and a scale multiplier set every firm's access to outside equity.
5. **proforma.ts:887-895**: the lease-up deficit integrates an asserted power curve that opens 20% let, while the lease-up clock is an exponential: two shapes for one lease-up.
6. **proforma.ts:525, 573-576**: ground-floor retail is gated on a demand score of 38 and on the retail/lead rent ratio against its opening value.
7. **amortize.ts:65 `CAP_PREMIUM_PCT = 0.0125`**: an option priced at a constant, so hedges are cheap when rates are volatile.
8. **streetLoans.ts:131-146, rivalDebt.ts:406-409**: the opening mortgage book is backdated but carries today's coupon, so the first decade has no refinancing cliff.
9. **streetLoans.ts:428**: balloon extensions are "twice, a year at a time" by rule, not the desk's extend-or-foreclose comparison.
10. **proforma.ts:776, 803**: the developer's rent and cap beliefs are clamped to 0.55-1.75 and 0.6-1.7, and the code's own comment quotes the 1.75 as a level reached.

Close behind: the appetite forms (proforma.ts:351, 740; pricing.ts:16-18) and the friends-and-family network rule (syndicate.ts:65, 330; fund.ts:654).
