Broadway & Wall: fewer words, more depth

Rules

  1. Word budget. Labels 1–3 words. No sentences on screen.
  2. Numbers, not sentences. Figures, deltas, sparklines, columns.
  3. Every number drills. Click any figure for its breakdown.
  4. Dense tables. 30px rows, every column sorts, sparkline columns.
  5. Fewest clicks. Defaults and keys for every frequent verb.
  6. No handholding. No onboarding, coaching or “start here”.
  7. No decorative motion. State changes are instant.
