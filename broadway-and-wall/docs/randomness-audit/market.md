## Summary

Counts, counting rows above plus grouped guard and physical sites:
- FAKE: 32 rows (8 high, 15 med or med-high, 9 low or low-med)
- EXOGENOUS: about 30 draw sites in 21 rows (one cosmetic draw breaks stream discipline)
- PHYSICAL: about 25 sites
- GUARD: about 60 sites (four flagged "verify it never binds": house user-cost floor 2222, demand LOG_CAP 1201, DRIFT/TOTAL_CAP, manufIdx floor)

Top 5 fakes to fix first:
1. **market.ts:5885 cityVac clamp 0.45** (and streetVac 5901). The printed vacancy disagrees with occupied/stock exactly when it matters (a glut), and every price reads it. It is a one-line fix, and the glut curve already saturates on its own (DEEP_RATE).
2. **market.ts:1634-1700 opening dearness R and q draws** (plus the P numeraire). The same map opens 5x apart in rent, glut against shortage, by seed. Derive q from the map's supply constraint and dearness from the regional spatial equilibrium.
3. **market.ts:5069 construction starts = 0.16% of stock × U(0.7,1.3)**. Supply, the other half of every rent and vacancy outcome, is an asserted rate times a die. Replace it with site-level pencils making an upward-sloping supply curve.
4. **market.ts:6912 landIdx formula plus die** (and 1733). This is the textbook fake CLAUDE.md names. Land must be a residual or comps-discovered.
5. **market.ts:6703 rent noise and 6857 cap noise** (one fix pattern). Monthly dice on rents and caps wherever the deal and comp tape is thin. Extend the deal and comp tape to industrial and aggregate flats, and use appraisal smoothing (no die) when the tape is thin.

(Next in line: the creditIdx 0.4 floor plus die, and pool truncation at housable + 0.25×natural, both load-bearing in deep cycles.)

Risky fixes:
- **Removing the 0.45 vacancy clamp**: very deep gluts will now print, so every downstream reader (concessions, caps' vacRisk = beta×gap×100 is unbounded, glut curve, lender stress) sees new territory. Run `pnpm stress` and overbuild scenarios. Expect BASELINE movement.
- **Opening R and q from the map**: it re-baselines every standing number and every seed. P as a numeraire is safe only if no un-scaled dollar constant remains, so audit `dollarLevel` usage first.
- **Site-level starts**: this changes the supply cycle's amplitude and period (the stated 5.3-year loop). Without the random spread, all sites clearing at once could synchronise booms. Use a per-site decision hazard, not a deterministic switch.
- **Land as residual**: residuals are highly levered (small rent moves, large land moves), and land feeds lot pricing, tax and developer bids. Expect volatility. Blend with comps.
- **Removing the pool cap / mainland margin**: the pool cap is what currently stops rents compounding in a shortage (per its comment). The replacement location margin must be in place in the same change, or real rents run away (the 5x-real-century fault the comments describe).
- **creditIdx without the floor**: credit can fall to near zero in a depression and shut both construction and refinancing. The lender-capital model must be checked to recover.
