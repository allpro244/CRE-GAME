Design proposal · for owner review · not built yet

Broadway & Wall, simpler and faster

Five places instead of fifteen, one drawer at a time beside the city instead of a room over it, and the jobs you do most cut to one to four clicks. Below: what was measured, then the eight core screens drawn over the Unreal city. Figures in the frames are example numbers from a test save (a 50-building firm in 2012), not your game.

Where everything goes

Every page that exists today keeps a home. Nothing is deleted; fifteen rail entries fold into five places, each with at most five tabs.

What changed

  • The HUD is one 52px row in ink, readable over any part of the city. Debt is on it.
  • One Inbox replaces the docket, the card queue and the at-risk banner. Cards still stop the clock when it matters, and “Later” files them here.

  • Island / Region scope switch reserves the place for the metro mode.

Clicks

  • Advance a month: 1 (Space)
  • Answer a tenant letter: 3 1

Why no redesign later

  • Scope is one value in the store (island or region). Every place reads it.
  • The Land lane, tract rows in Market and land rows in Holdings reuse the stage board, table and building-file components.
  • Homebuilder firms appear under City › Players, the same as rival landlords.

Open question for the owner

  • Should the region view be a 3D metro scene in Unreal, or a 2D map drawn by the page (cheaper, ready sooner)? The layout works for both.
