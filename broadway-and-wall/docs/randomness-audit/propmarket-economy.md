# Fake-number audit: propmarket.ts, economy.ts, households.ts, and the engine files no table covered

Verified against main at c75ef8b (#838), 2026-11-20.

Read-only first pass against broadway-and-wall/CLAUDE.md "NO FAKE NUMBERS" and "Randomness: only at the boundary of the model". Paths are relative to `broadway-and-wall/src/engine/`. Line numbers are from the working tree: HEAD c184d44 (the opening-dearness change), mid-merge of `main` (MERGE_HEAD c75ef8b), the same tree market.md was checked on. Nothing was run. "Binds?" claims come from the code's own comments or from arithmetic. Where a row says "unverified binding", no bind rate has been measured.

market.md says caps and land moved to propmarket.ts (`tickCapRead`, `tickLandRead`) and jobs, migration and industry shocks moved to economy.ts and households.ts, and that "those files were not part of this audit". This file covers them.

Randomness enters here through: the economy stream (economy.ts:308 `stream`, a mulberry32 copy on `s.streams.economy`, `R.u()`/`R.n()`); the households stream (households.ts:1012, `R.u()`/`R.n()`, `pick` at 1031); `hashU01` (economy.ts:1913, Calvo repricing); `hash01` copies (competitorTraits.ts, broker.ts:40, inputs.ts:84, ownership.ts:70, setup.ts:192, takeprivate.ts:61); `hashNormal` (contractors.ts:121); `rrange` (acquire.ts); `rng(s, "citygov")` (citygov.ts). propmarket.ts makes no draw of its own. It reads one: `misreadPp`, which is a hash in competitorTraits.ts.

How the households register is read. households.ts is an agent microsimulation. Most of its roughly 90 draws are Bernoulli trials at a computed or sourced hazard (separation, finding, death, birth, union, move) or a pick of which household, firm or flat. These are counted as **EXOGENOUS (who)**: the rate is the model's, and the draw only chooses which agent. That is the use CLAUDE.md allows ("randomness may choose who"). They are grouped below rather than listed one by one. A draw is FAKE where the **rate or the size itself** is unsourced, [K], or held fixed against the model.
