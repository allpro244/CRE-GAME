## Guards binding on economic quantities

A guard that fires is a value the model computed out of range — a negative rent, a share above one. These may be hiding faults upstream.

Most rows here are hinges, `max(0, x - k)`: a response that starts at a threshold, which is arithmetic doing its job when x below k is normal (a delinquency rate under its trigger, an index rate below where a lender gets nervous). A row is a suspect when the clipped quantity should never have been negative at all — a rent, a value, an NOI.
