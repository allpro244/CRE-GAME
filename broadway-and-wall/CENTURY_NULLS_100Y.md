## Method

- Engine: null player (`newGame` + `advanceMonth`), no strategy bot.
- Same city geometry for every market seed; market seed drives RNG streams.
- Real series = nominal / CPI.
- Land $/sf is a 400-parcel sample mean of `landValue / lotArea`.
- If you re-run: `YEARS=100 SEEDS=6 CITY_SEED=20261 node tools/century-report.mjs`.
